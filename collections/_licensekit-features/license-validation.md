## License Validation

[LicenseKit](/licensekit) can read and validate licenses in many ways. Licenses can be compiled into the product binary, parsed from files, or fetched from a remote API, then validated for granular access control.

LicenseKit licenses that can define expiration date, features, tier, bundle IDs, supported platforms & environments, and much more.

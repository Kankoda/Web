## Encrypted License Files

[LicenseKit](/sdks/licensekit) can write licenses into encrypted files, which makes it easy for you to distribute licenses in encrypted files to your customers.

Since encrypted license files require manual work, they are best reserved for customers that pay for a full year. You may also consider requiring a certain tier for this service.

You can choose if a license should expire on its expiration date, or if you want your customer to be able to keep using it in all versions where the license was active.

## License Files

[LicenseKit](/sdks/licensekit) can generate and read encrypted license files, which makes it easy for you to distribute licenses to your customers.

Since encrypted license files require manual work and are hard to revoke, they are best suited for customers that pay for a long period.

You can choose if a license should expire on its expiration date, or if you want your customer to be able to keep using it in all versions where the license was active.

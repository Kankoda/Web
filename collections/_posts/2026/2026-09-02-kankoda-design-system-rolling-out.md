The new Kankoda web design system is being rolled out, and is currently actively used on four websites - this main one, and the company's [KeyboardKit]({{page.keyboardkit}}), [Appamini]({{page.appamini}}), and [Wally]({{page.wally}}) product sites.


## KeyboardKit

![KeyboardKit logo]({{page.assets}}keyboardkit.jpg)

Being the product site of an SDK, the [KeyboardKit]({{page.keyboardkit}}) sticks with the same visual style as this website. The design system's built-in sync capabilities makes it easy to sync changes in both directions.


## Wally

![Wally logo]({{page.assets}}wally.jpg)

Since the [Wally app]({{page.wally}}) has a distinct, skeumorphic design, we decided to tweak the color scheme to dark, apply a background skin texture, and apply the app's signature font, while keeping the overall layout.

This makes the resulting Wally website fit right in with the brand guidelines, while getting a very distinct look that clearly separates it from the rest.


## Appamini

![Appamini logo]({{page.assets}}appamini.jpg)

Just like Wally, the [Appamini apps]({{page.wally}}) have a distinct, playful design, that we wanted reflected in the new website. We therefore kept most things intact, but added clouds to the top, and applied a handwriting font.

This makes the resulting Appamini website fit right in with the brand guidelines, while getting a distinct look. We are very happy with it.


## Remaining Work

Since we have gathered most of our [apps](/apps) and [SDKs](/sdks) on this website, we don't have the same amount of sites as before. We therefore only have the [Daniel Saidi]({{page.danielsaidi}}) website left to migrate.

This will be quite an undertaking, since it involves migrating a *lot* of pages, including many completely separate designs. We may therefore decide to make them into separate sub-domains as part of this work.

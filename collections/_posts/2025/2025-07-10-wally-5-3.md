{{page.version}} is out, with support for custom fonts, a new denim skin, and a corduroy skin collection! It also makes it easier to customize the main menu.

![Blog post header]({{page.image}})

{{page.version}} lets you pick a custom font for navigation titles and decorative texts. You can pick between two hand-drawn fonts, a font for dyslexia, and all system fonts.

This version also adds a new denim skin and a brand new corduroy skin collection with four skins.

You can read more about this update on the [Wally website](https://wally.app/blog/2025/07/10/wally-5-3)
